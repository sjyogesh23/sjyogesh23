I'm happiest when an idea leaves the notebook and becomes something people actually use.

My journey started in software engineering, building and deploying applications on AWS. Curiosity pulled me into AI, and I brought that engineering mindset with me: a model is only half the story until it runs reliably in production.

Along the way, my research became a granted UK patent and publications. I've won hackathons, and I host webinars, because explaining an idea is the best way to truly understand it.
